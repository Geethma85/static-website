What is Codacs all about?

Codacs is an online platform offering interactive tracks in web dev, systems programming, and computer science fundamentals.

Is Codacs free or paid?

We offer both free essential tracks and premium certifications that include direct code reviews and live mentorship.

How long will it take to complete a track?

All material is self-paced. Most students finish a full learning path within 6 to 12 weeks with 5 hours of study per week.

Will I receive a verified certificate?

Yes, completing project checkpoints and passing final assessments unlocks an industry-ready certificate.

Can I study on my phone or tablet?

Yes, the platform is fully responsive so you can read lectures, review snippets, and track progress on mobile.
